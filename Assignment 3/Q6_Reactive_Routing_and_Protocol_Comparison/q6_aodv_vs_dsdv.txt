Q6 - AODV Route Discovery (RREQ / RREP)
S broadcasts RREQ for D
S forwards RREQ to A
S forwards RREQ to B
A forwards RREQ to D
B forwards RREQ to C
D returns RREP along reverse path: D -> A -> S
Installed route: S -> A -> D

AODV vs DSDV summary:
AODV: reactive; route discovery occurs on demand; control messages follow route requests/failures.
DSDV: proactive; routing tables are updated continuously; routes may be immediately available.
Selection: AODV is a reasonable choice for a frequently changing network with intermittent traffic.
Note: Graph-level teaching example, not a full network simulation.

4B TASK 6 - BATTERY-AWARE SYSTEM MODEL
Normal Wi-Fi => NORMAL sampling; batch upload
Low battery => CRITICAL; minimum safe sampling; defer unless urgent
Offline => OFFLINE; local queue; sync after reconnect
Emergency => EMERGENCY; high-priority alert immediately

VALIDATION
Normal: batch upload on Wi-Fi. Low: reduce non-urgent work.
Offline: persist records and retry idempotently after connection returns.
Emergency: override power-saving delay for priority alert.
5,000 devices: server queues, partitions, rate limits, jittered retry and priority lanes.

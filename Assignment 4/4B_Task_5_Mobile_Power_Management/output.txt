4B TASK 5 - POWER MANAGEMENT
DVFS / heterogeneous cores: CPU utilisation and thermal state; frequency/voltage; lower dynamic power
Doze / App Standby: screen off, stationary, unplugged; defer jobs/network to maintenance windows
Application batching: non-urgent samples or uploads; fewer wakeups/radio activations
Short wakelock: bounded critical operation; long locks drain battery

STRESS: low battery + intermittent network
Increase sample interval, batch uploads, use bounded retry/backoff, preserve emergency override.
Energy estimates require device-current/voltage or profiler evidence; no physical measurement is fabricated.
